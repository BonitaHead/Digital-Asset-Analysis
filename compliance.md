## AML/KYC Framework

### KYC / Customer Due Diligence

Core concepts include identity verification, customer risk profiling, expected activity, beneficial ownership where applicable, ongoing monitoring, and escalation of unusual activity.

### Transaction Monitoring

Blockchain analytics can complement traditional monitoring by examining observable transaction behavior, counterparties, asset movements, and exposure to relevant infrastructure.

### Risk Indicators

Potential indicators can include:

- Unusual transaction size or frequency relative to expected activity
- Rapid movement across multiple addresses
- Structuring or fragmentation patterns
- Unusual cross-chain movement
- Exposure to high-risk services or counterparties when supported by reliable intelligence
- Rapid deposit/withdrawal patterns involving exchange infrastructure
- Activity inconsistent with a known customer profile

An indicator is a reason for additional review—not proof of criminal activity.
