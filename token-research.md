# Token Research & Tokenomics

## Purpose

This section evaluates digital-asset tokens and protocols using a structured research framework covering supply, distribution, utility, liquidity, governance, incentives, and risk.

## Token Research Framework

**Project/Protocol → Token Utility → Supply → Distribution → Liquidity → Incentives → Governance → Risks → Evidence Quality**

### Core Metrics

- Maximum supply
- Total supply
- Circulating supply
- Emission or issuance schedule
- Token distribution
- Unlock schedules where documented
- Liquidity sources
- Trading venues
- Utility and demand drivers
- Governance mechanisms
- Staking/reward mechanisms

## Risk Categories

### Market Risk
Volatility, concentration, liquidity, and market-depth considerations.

### Tokenomics Risk
Supply expansion, concentrated ownership, unlock events, emissions, and incentive sustainability.

### Protocol Risk
Smart-contract, bridge, oracle, governance, and dependency risks where applicable.

### Compliance / AML Context
Token research can help establish the asset and protocol context surrounding a transaction-monitoring investigation. Token characteristics alone do not establish illicit activity.

## Evidence Standard

Each case study should identify primary project documentation where available, blockchain data sources, retrieval dates, calculations, assumptions, and limitations. Market-price information should be time-stamped because digital-asset markets change rapidly.
