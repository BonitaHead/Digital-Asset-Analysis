# Digital-Asset Market Analysis

## Purpose

This section applies financial-market and risk-analysis concepts to digital assets. It connects traditional market structure and trading concepts with cryptocurrency market behavior without treating market observations as investment recommendations.

## Analytical Areas

- Market structure
- Price and volume behavior
- Liquidity and market depth
- Volatility
- Trading activity
- Exchange and DEX market structure
- Token supply and circulating liquidity
- Market and protocol risk

## Research Framework

**Market Question → Data → Market Structure → Quantitative/Qualitative Analysis → Risk Interpretation → Limitations**

### Data to Capture

- Asset and trading pair
- Observation period
- Price and volume data
- Liquidity/depth where available
- Exchange or protocol source
- Relevant token supply information
- Market-event context

## Financial-Markets Context

Online Trading Academy (OTA) training in foreign-exchange markets provides background in market analysis, trading principles, risk management, and financial-market mechanics. In this portfolio, that knowledge is applied as analytical context rather than as a claim of professional trading employment.

## Risk Considerations

Digital-asset markets can exhibit high volatility, fragmented liquidity, market-structure differences, smart-contract exposure, counterparty risk, and rapid changes in token supply or protocol conditions. Analysis should identify the relevant risk rather than predict price direction.
