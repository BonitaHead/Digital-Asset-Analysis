# Digital Asset Analysis

## Blockchain Analytics | AML/KYC Risk Research | On-Chain Intelligence

This repository contains structured research and analytical case studies focused on digital assets, blockchain transactions, wallets, market activity, tokenomics, and compliance-oriented risk analysis.

The objective is to demonstrate a repeatable analytical workflow:

**Research Question → Data/Evidence → Methodology → Findings → Risk Interpretation → Limitations → Sources**

### Research Areas

- Blockchain transaction analysis
- Wallet and address behavior
- Digital-asset AML/KYC concepts
- Transaction monitoring and risk indicators
- On-chain behavioral analysis
- Digital-asset market structure
- Token research and tokenomics
- DeFi and decentralized-exchange activity

### Analytical Principles

1. **Separate evidence from inference.** On-chain observations do not automatically establish real-world identity or intent.
2. **Use a risk-based approach.** An indicator may warrant investigation without proving illicit activity.
3. **Document limitations.** Attribution and behavioral conclusions require appropriate caveats.
4. **Prefer reproducibility.** Research should identify data sources, methods, assumptions, and dates.
5. **Protect privacy.** Case studies use public blockchain data and avoid unnecessary personal information.
