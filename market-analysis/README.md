# Digital-Asset Market Analysis

Financial-market and risk analysis applied to digital assets, connecting market structure, liquidity, volatility, trading activity, and token supply with disciplined risk interpretation.

## Financial-Markets Context

Online Trading Academy (OTA) Forex training provides background in market mechanics, trading principles, market analysis, and risk management. This portfolio uses that knowledge as analytical context rather than claiming professional trading employment.
